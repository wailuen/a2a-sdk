# KPI briefing

When asked for a "briefing" or "overview":

1. Call `demo_kpis` with a title naming the period (e.g. "Q2 2026 KPIs").
2. Call `demo_trend` for the headline metric to show its trajectory.
3. Summarize in two sentences: what moved, and why it matters.

Never invent numbers — every figure must come from a tool result.
